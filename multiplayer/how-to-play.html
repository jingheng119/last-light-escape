HOW TO PLAY

Find the light. Restore the systems. Survive the Hollow. Everything you need before entering the ward.

Escape each floor

Every floor is a maze with objectives to complete before extraction.

  1. Collect power cores scattered throughout the maze.
  2. Rescue the trapped survivor by interacting nearby.
  3. Activate the relay to restore the exit systems.
  4. Reach the elevator once the objectives are complete. In co-op, gather your team at the exit.
You are here to escape, not to eliminate every Hollow. Use the direction indicator to locate your next objective.

Know the threats

Normal Hollow

Pursues survivors. Break line of sight and use cover to escape.

Dasher

Chases and lunges in bursts. Avoid standing directly in its approach.

Hunter

A persistent threat. Stay alert and keep an escape route ready.

Ghost

Moves in patrol patterns. Watch its path before crossing.

Invisible Hollow

Can vanish temporarily. Keep moving and watch the area around you.

Choose your role

Each survivor has a unique skill activated with F. Your selection is locked during an active run.

Scout

Stun nearby Hollows with a shockwave.

Medic

Heal yourself or a teammate.

Invisibler

Vanish from enemy sight for three seconds.

Teleporter

Activate Phase Guard to teleport when struck.

Illusioner

Send a moving phantom to distract Hollows.

Rewinder

Rewind a targeted ally or Hollow by three seconds.

Anchor

Place an anchor, then activate again to return through walls.

Symbiote

Attach to a teammate to boost their walking speed.

Survive together

Create a room or join one using an invite code. Rooms support up to five players.

  1. Choose your nickname and role before joining.
  2. The host starts the match after everyone is ready.
  3. Work together to finish objectives and gather at the elevator.
  4. To revive a fallen teammate, stand near their soul for five seconds.
  5. Use team chat to coordinate. Players joining an already-started match become spectators.
Protect your teammates: an escape is a team effort.

Three stages. Twenty-seven floors.

Progress through three stages of nine floors each. The mazes expand and the rescue objectives grow as you descend. Dimensions are measured in maze tiles.

The Copper Engine

Venture through a vast industrial labyrinth packed with copper machinery, fast Dashers, and a relentless Hunter. Bigger maps require careful teamwork.

STAGE 03 · FLOORS 19–27

The Lost Signal

Enter the largest and darkest sectors, where Invisible Hollows and roaming Ghosts distort the path ahead. Complete the final escape together.

Dimensions51 × 41
Allies to save3
Power cores6–8

Special ally: rescue the Echo. Each teammate gets one automatic five-second ghost transformation that blocks a hit and passes through walls.

Every floor requires its power cores, rescued allies, and an activated relay before the elevator opens. All living teammates must reach the exit.
