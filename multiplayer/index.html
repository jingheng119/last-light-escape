Stage 4: The White Room

A new chapter is taking shape. Beyond the familiar corridors, survivors will enter the White Room — an unfamiliar environment where the rules of survival may feel very different. The next stage is on the way, and this time, the unknown may be closer than ever.

  • A new place to survive: prepare to explore the White Room, a distinct setting that will take the journey beyond the first three stages.
